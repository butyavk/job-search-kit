<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Search and rank" icon="magnifier">
    LinkedIn, company boards through ATS APIs, job sites and country boards. Every vacancy is checked two ways and passes a five-point gate as a screener sees it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apply through people" icon="comment">
    The hiring manager, the recruiter, people you know there. A CV tailored by selection, the letter, the form filled — submitted only on your yes for that form.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Track and follow up" icon="list-format">
    The open-source tracker-mcp: every write previewed and reversible, follow-up dates, funnel, calendar export. A daily check every morning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prepare and practise" icon="star">
    Answers built from your own checked sentences, likely questions per stage, system design and live coding training, mocks and same-day debriefs.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Built from a real two-month search for engineering-manager roles: about a hundred applications, interviews up to final rounds, and the numbers behind every rule. Your personal data stays in a separate pack that never goes into the repository.
