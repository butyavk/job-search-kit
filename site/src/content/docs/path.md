| Step | Command | What Claude does | What stays with you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Start | `/start` | Builds your pack from your CV and a few interview sessions. Proposes roles, levels, countries, remote or relocation, and money floors from the market. Sets up the tracker and the schedule. | choose from the options, confirm facts |
| 2. Search | `/find` | Reads LinkedIn, company boards through ATS APIs, job sites and country boards; checks each vacancy two ways; runs the five-point gate; ranks the rest. A short list goes into the tracker. | choose where to apply |
| 3. People | `/people <company>` | Finds the hiring manager, the recruiter, people you know there, a referral path; drafts the notes. | send them |
| 4. Apply | `/apply <vacancy>` | The gate, a CV tailored with a keyword table, the letter, form answers; fills the form without submitting; plans the note and the follow-ups. | press «submit», say «applied» |
| 5. Every day | `/today` (and on a schedule) | Mail and LinkedIn into the tracker, new vacancies from alerts, follow-ups due with drafts, interviews this week. | send the drafts |
| 6. A stage | `/prep <company> <stage>` | Format and people, likely questions with answers from your blocks, a trainer for the format, a plan by days. | practise |
| 7. Mock | `/mock <kind>` | Recruiter, manager, behavioral, system design or live coding — by voice or text, with an honest debrief. | do the mock |
| 8. After a call | `/debrief` | What happened and the next step; new facts into your pack; reminders. | tell how it went |
| 9. Monthly | `/review` | Funnel, rejection analysis, what to change in the frame, the CV or the sources; a pack and fact check. | agree to the changes |

Everything else is plain words: «compose an answer to B03», «add a fact», «X rejected me», «I applied to Y».

## What runs by itself
The daily check every morning (a push only when there is something to do), a weekly search pass, a monthly review — as scheduled tasks where your Claude app has them. Follow-up dates also go to your calendar from the tracker.

## Training
- System design: the round's 6-step frame, questions before drawing, mocks as the interviewer; theory on [System Design Map](https://butyavk.github.io/system-design/).
- Live coding: the 25-minute protocol and timed mocks; tasks and in-browser checking on [Live Coding Map](https://butyavk.github.io/live-coding/).
- A trainer for one company's coding stage when its domain or stack is not covered.
