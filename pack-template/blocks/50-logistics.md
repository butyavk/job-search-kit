# Logistics and questions for the interviewer

Topics LOG and ASK have IDs without a type letter (`LOG-1`, `ASK-1`): they are one-line templates, not stories. The number for LOG-6 comes from `profile.yaml` → `money.say_aloud`; the floor (`money.floor`) stays private by default. A candidate who stays flexible (`money.approach`) starts with LOG-7 and lets the recruiter name the range first.

#### LOG-1 · Where I am
`O` · location · ~6 s · draft
- EN: I am based in <city>, UTC <offset>. <remote / relocation sentence>
- XX: <…>

#### LOG-2 · Work authorization
`O` · work authorization · ~5 s · draft
- EN: I have the right to work in <country>. <For other countries: I would need sponsorship / I work as a contractor from <country>.>
- XX: <…>

#### LOG-3 · Contract form
`O` · contract form · ~5 s · draft
- EN: <I am open to employment or a contractor agreement / I work as a contractor through my own company>.
- XX: <…>

#### LOG-4 · When I can start
`O` · start date · ~2 s · draft
- EN: I can start <immediately / in N weeks>.
- XX: <…>

#### LOG-6 · Money
`O` · salary · ~8 s · draft
- EN: I am looking at about <number> a <month/year>, gross. <If the posting has a range: The posted range works for me.>
- XX: <…>

#### LOG-7 · Their range first
`O` · salary · ~5 s · draft
- EN: I am flexible on the number. What range do you have for this role?
- XX: <…>

#### LOG-8 · Their range works
`O` · salary · ~6 s · draft
- EN: Your range works for me. The salary is not the most important part for me; it depends on <the scope, the team, the product>. I am very flexible.
- XX: <…>

`O` · questions to ask · draft
- EN: What does the team own today, and what would the new person take on first?
