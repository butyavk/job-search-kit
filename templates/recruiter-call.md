# The recruiter call

## What to find out
- Stages, length, who the interviewers are, when the decision comes.
- Work format: country of employment, contract or EOR, time zones.
- The range, if not published (the recruiter asks or tells; the candidate's own rules are in `me/profile.yaml` → `money` and `me/never-say.md`).
- Interview rules: recording, AI tools, what platform live coding is on.
- The company's values and how they are assessed, if not published.

## What to say (prepare in advance)
- A 60-second intro and «why this role» (recipes I01, I02).
- Money expectations — by the candidate's rules (block LOG-6).
- Notice period and contract form — from the profile.
- Where the candidate is and the hours they can work.

## After the call
Everything into the context file: stages with dates, people, rules. The tracker: status and next step.
