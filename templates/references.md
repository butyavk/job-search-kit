# References

Usually 1–2 current or recent managers, or a colleague. Choose them once the process passes the first screen, and warn them before the final round.

Collect from each: name; title then and now; company; how and when you worked together; email; time zone; whether they can speak English.

## The request message (template, in the language you use with them)
> Hi <name>! I am in the last stages at <company> for the <role> role. The last step is references. May I give them your contact? We worked together at <company> on <project>.
> If they contact you, it is usually a short call or an email: how we worked, what I do well and what I should develop.
> If you agree, please send me your email, title and time zone, and I will send you a short memo with dates. If it is not a good time for you, that is completely fine.

## The memo for a reference
It refreshes dates and facts; it is not a script: the reference speaks in their own words and only about what they saw.
- Roles and dates (from the CV).
- 3–4 projects this person saw closely (only real authorship).
- What matters to the company (values), for context.
- How to answer: examples are better than ratings; if you do not remember a number, say so.

## If they ask why not your last manager
Only facts about who saw the work and when. No judgement of the former manager.
