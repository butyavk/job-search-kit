# Learning sheet — <company>, <stage>, <date and time>

The prep document is the reserve; this sheet is what you learn. You learn anchors and first sentences, not texts: the words in the interview will be your own, the facts will be the same.

## How to learn
1. **Stories, not answers.** Almost any question is a first sentence plus one of the stories below.
2. **Recall, not rereading.** Read an anchor once, close it, tell it aloud.
3. **Aloud, timed:** 60–90 seconds an answer, a case up to 60. Over time — cut the situation, never the number.
4. **Shuffled:** the interviewer will not follow this page.
5. **Only the misses again:** ✗ — the same evening and the next morning; ✓ two days running — out of the drill.
6. **Freeze** two days before: nothing new, only drills.

The drill: `/mock drill <company>` (or the words «drill <company>»). Claude asks in random order, marks each answer and writes the marks below.

## Positions (no story)
Views, plans and frames the stage asks for — a 90-day plan, metrics, «how I see the role». Learned by their numbers: «three steps», «four hypotheses», one word each.

## Drill plan
- [ ] Day 1 — read each anchor once; tell each story aloud twice with the sheet closed; the pitch three times on a timer.
- [ ] Each next day — `/mock drill`, 15–25 minutes; in the evening only the ✗.
- [ ] Two days before — a full mock at the hour of the interview; then freeze.
- [ ] The day before — the two weakest answers, the pitch and money aloud on a timer.
- [ ] The interview day — rewrite the map by hand from memory (first sentences only) and the two questions; logistics.

## Marks
✓ — the first sentence and all four steps, no prompt, in time · ~ — a pause, too long, or no number · ✗ — not recalled or off the point.
