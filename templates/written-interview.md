# A written interview — how to work

Some companies start with written answers to 20–35 questions (experience, management, education, «why us»), and every later interview refers back to them. Often with the condition «write in your own words, AI-generated responses cannot be accepted».

## Division of labour
- The candidate writes the English text. Claude gives, per question: what they want to see, a plan in the candidate's language with facts from the pack, what is missing, the length; then it checks what was written.
- If the AI condition is there, Claude does not write the finished English. Say it once and do not come back to it.

## The working document
Per question, word for word: «what they want to see» → a plan with facts → questions for the candidate → length → a «My answer» field. Separately: all questions for the candidate in blocks, a story map (which story in which answer, no repeats), differences from the sent CV and form, checked numbers.

## Steps
1. Research the company and the role, with sources — before the questions.
2. Work by blocks: start where there is most material (management, context); for education and biography collect facts first, invent nothing.
3. Multi-part questions — check each part separately, or a part reads as skipped.
4. Check what was written: facts against the pack; every part of the question; repeats between answers; the register script (`english-register`).
5. Anonymity, if the review is anonymous: no name, contacts or profile links in the text or in the PDF metadata (Title, Author). A neutral file name; check with `pdfinfo` and `pdftotext`.
6. After submitting — the tracker, and a document «what was written — the basis for interviews»: what exactly was said about weak points, which examples were used, what was left out.

## «Why us» and «what would you change»
Own reasons may not come at once — ask Claude for 2–3 options on different grounds (mission, horizon, way of working), choose yourself and back it with a fact from your experience. Name the company's risks calmly and concretely, with a source.
