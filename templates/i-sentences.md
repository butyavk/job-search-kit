# I-sentences — card

The first or second sentence of any answer about role and contribution: «I + verb + a result with a number». Fill 8–12 rows from your blocks; say each aloud until it comes without a pause.

| Interviewer's trigger | I-sentence (in the interview language) | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tell me about your role at <company> | I led <N> engineers in <M> teams through their leads, and I … | |
| What did you build yourself? | I traced <the problem> myself and made <what> <N times> faster. | |
| How do you hire? | I hire in three steps: … | |
| … | … | |

Rules: «I», not «we», when you really did it; «we» when it was the team, and then at once «my part was…». A number only if it is in `me/facts.md`, with how it was measured.
